# Remokey — PC Remote Control

Control your Windows PC from your Android phone: volume, media, touchpad & keyboard, screen view, files, apps and power — over Wi-Fi or the internet.

🇬🇧 English · 🇷🇺 [Русский](#русский)

## Download
- **Windows 10/11 program:** [latest release](https://github.com/ggdi5bapp-rgb/remokey/releases/latest) → `Remokey.exe`
- **Android app:** Google Play (in testing now — public release coming soon)

## Getting started
1. Run `Remokey.exe` on your PC.
2. Connect the phone and the PC to the same Wi-Fi.
3. In the app: **Settings → Add PC → Find PC on network**, enter the PIN and click **Allow** on the PC.

## Security & privacy
- Pairing is approved on the PC itself; all traffic is end-to-end encrypted (ChaCha20-Poly1305).
- No ads, no accounts, no data collection. [Privacy policy](https://ggdi5bapp-rgb.github.io/remokey/privacy-policy.html)
- Separate permissions for every phone: *Full*, *Remote* or *Guest*.

## Antivirus note
Some AI/heuristic antivirus engines may flag Remokey as suspicious because it is a remote-control tool. Microsoft Defender does not flag it. Checksums (`SHA256SUMS.txt`) are attached to every release.
